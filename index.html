About

My passion is designing software for places where the screen is only part of the story: a barn, a clinic, a farm office.

What’s happening around the person using it matters as much as what’s on it. That’s what draws me to agritech, IoT and products connected to the physical world.

Over the past 12 years, I’ve worked on complex B2B systems across agriculture, healthcare, media, fintech and security: plenty of dashboards, dense tables and workflows that needed untangling.

My favorite part is figuring out what a product could be. I explore different ways it could work, not just different ways it could look, and make those directions concrete enough to discuss and compare. Once we choose one, I stay close to product managers and developers through the details of building it.

AI has become part of that hands-on process. I use it to build working prototypes in code, so I can try out the behavior, timing and small interactions that static screens leave open to interpretation. I also structure design systems so both people and AI tools can work with them.
