About

I’m Eilat, a designer who starts every project with a concept and finishes it with care.

For more than a decade I’ve designed complex B2B systems, dashboards and data-heavy interfaces, across agritech, healthcare, media, fintech and security. I’m most drawn to products that meet the physical world, like agritech and IoT, where a screen has to work for people in a barn, a clinic or a control room.

My strength is concepts. I present several truly different directions before we choose, then carry the chosen one all the way to development, working closely with product managers and developers.

AI is a core part of my process: I build working prototypes in code, including micro-interactions and motion, and set up design systems that work well with AI.
